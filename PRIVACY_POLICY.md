# NebRatings Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 8, 2026**

NebRatings ("we," "us," or "the app") is a movie and TV rating app. This
policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices
you have. By creating an account or using NebRatings, you agree to this policy.

We keep this deliberately short and plain: **we do not sell your data, we do
not show ads, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics
trackers.**

## Information we collect

**Account information.** When you sign up, we collect your email address and a
password so we can create and secure your account. Your password is handled by
Google Firebase Authentication and is stored in a hashed form we cannot read.

**Profile information.** Information you choose to add to your profile, such as
your username, avatar, bio, favorite genres, and favorite movie or show.

**Content you create.** The ratings ("nebs"), written reviews, lists,
reactions, and friend connections you make in the app. Reviews, ratings, and
public lists are visible to other NebRatings users; your email address is
never shown to other users.

**Support messages.** If you contact us through the app or by email, we receive
the message and your email address so we can respond.

We do **not** collect your contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers
(IDFA), or browsing activity outside the app.

## How we use your information

- To create and maintain your account and sign you in across devices.
- To store and display your ratings, reviews, lists, and profile.
- To power social features you opt into — friends, the activity feed, taste
  compatibility, and shared lists.
- To personalize recommendations (for example, by mood, favorite genres, and
  your content-maturity preference).
- To respond to support requests.

## Content maturity and age-appropriate use

NebRatings filters out explicit adult content and never recommends it. You can
set a content-maturity level (Family Friendly, General Audience, or No
Restrictions) that controls the recommendations you see.

NebRatings is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has
provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## How your information is stored and shared

Your data is stored using **Google Firebase** (Firebase Authentication and
Cloud Firestore), which processes it on our behalf under Google's security and
privacy practices.

Movie and TV information (titles, artwork, cast, trailers) is provided by **The
Movie Database (TMDB)**. When the app loads this content, requests go to TMDB
and its image servers; these requests are not tied to your NebRatings identity.
This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with the
service providers above as needed to run the app, or if required by law.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can delete
your account at any time from **Settings → Account**. Deleting your account
permanently removes your profile, reviews, ratings, and lists from our active
systems. You can also email us to request deletion.

## Your choices

- Edit or delete your reviews, ratings, and lists at any time in the app.
- Update or remove profile details, including your favorites and bio.
- Change your content-maturity level in Settings.
- Delete your account to remove your data.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the
"Last updated" date above. Significant changes will be communicated in the app
or by email where appropriate.
