# NebRatings Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 8, 2026**
**Last updated: July 8, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how NebRatings ("**NebRatings**," "**we**," "**us**,"
or "**our**") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your information when you
use the NebRatings mobile application (the "**App**") and the website at
nebratings.com (together, the "**Service**").

NebRatings is a movie and TV rating app that lets you rate titles, write
reviews, build lists, discover what to watch, and compare tastes with friends.
By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

We have tried to keep this policy clear. The short version: **we do not sell
your personal information, we do not show third-party ads, and we do not use
advertising or analytics trackers.**

The Service is operated by the developer of NebRatings ("we"). For the purposes

**Contact:** nebratings@gmail.com

## 2. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information.

### 2.1 Information you provide to us

- **Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your **email
  address** and a **password**. Your password is managed by Google Firebase
  Authentication and is stored only in a securely hashed form that we cannot
  read.
- **Profile information.** Information you choose to add, such as your
  **username**, **avatar** (an emoji you select), **bio**, **favorite genres**,
  and **favorite movie or show**.
- **Content you create.** Your **ratings ("nebs")**, **written reviews**,
  **lists**, **reactions**, and **friend connections**.
- **Preferences.** Settings you choose, such as your **content-maturity
  preference** (Family Friendly, General Audience, or No Restrictions) and
  appearance settings.
- **Support communications.** If you contact us through the App or by email, we
  receive your message, your email address, and any information you include.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

When you use the Service, certain technical information is processed by our
infrastructure providers to operate and secure the Service, including:

- **Device and connection data**, such as IP address, device type and model,
  operating-system version, and app version.
- **Log and diagnostic data**, such as timestamps of actions (for example, when
  a review is created) and error/crash information used to keep the App working.
- **Approximate location**, which may be inferred from your IP address for
  security and abuse-prevention purposes. We do **not** collect precise (GPS)
  location.

We do **not** collect your device contacts, photos, microphone, or advertising
identifier (IDFA), and we do not track you across other companies' apps or
websites.

### 2.3 Information from third parties

Movie and TV information (titles, artwork, cast, and trailers) is supplied by
**The Movie Database (TMDB)**. This is content data about films and shows, not
personal information about you.

## 3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

- Create, secure, and maintain your account and sign you in across devices;
- Store and display your ratings, reviews, lists, reactions, and profile;
- Provide social features you choose to use, such as friends, the activity
  feed, taste-compatibility comparisons, "Watch Together" group
  recommendations, and shared lists;
- Personalize recommendations (for example, by mood, favorite genres, and your
  content-maturity preference);
- Filter out explicit adult content and enforce your content-maturity setting;
- Respond to your support requests;
- Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security incidents, and technical
  issues; and
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

- **Performance of a contract** — to provide the Service you request (for
  example, creating your account and storing your reviews).
- **Consent** — where you have given it (for example, submitting optional
  profile details). You may withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legitimate interests** — to keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and
  improve reliability, in a way that does not override your rights.
- **Legal obligation** — where we must process information to comply with
  applicable law.

## 5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

- **Service providers (sub-processors).** We use trusted providers to run the
  Service, who process information on our behalf under contractual protections:
  - **Google Firebase** (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) — account
    authentication and cloud database/storage of your profile and content.
  - **Apple** — app distribution and, where you enable it at the operating
    system level, standard app diagnostics.
  - **The Movie Database (TMDB)** — provides film and TV content. When the App
    loads this content, requests (including your IP address) go to TMDB and its
    image servers; these requests are not tied to your NebRatings account
    identity.
- **Other users.** Content you make public — such as reviews, ratings,
  usernames, avatars, and public lists — is visible to other NebRatings users.
  Your email address and password are never shown to other users. You control
  the visibility of each list (public, friends-only, or private).
- **Legal and safety.** We may disclose information if required by law, legal
  process, or governmental request, or where necessary to protect the rights,
  property, or safety of NebRatings, our users, or the public.
- **Business transfers.** If NebRatings is involved in a merger, acquisition,
  or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that
  transaction; we will notify you of any change in how your information is
  handled.

We do **not** sell your personal information and do **not** "share" it for
cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under
California law.

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including

## 9. Your rights and choices

**In the App, you can at any time:**

- Edit or delete your reviews, ratings, and lists;
- Update or remove profile details, including your bio and favorites;
- Change your content-maturity preference;
- Sign out; and
- **Delete your account** (Settings → Account), which permanently removes your
  associated content from our active systems.

**EU/UK (GDPR) rights.** If you are in the EEA or UK, you have the right to
access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our processing of your personal
information; the right to data portability; and the right to withdraw consent.
You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection
authority.

**California (CCPA/CPRA) rights.** If you are a California resident, you have the
right to know what personal information we collect and how we use and disclose
it; the right to delete it; the right to correct it; and the right not to be
discriminated against for exercising your rights. We do not sell or share your
personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is
nothing to opt out of in that respect.

**Exercising your rights.** You can exercise most rights directly in the App. To
make any other request, email us at **nebratings@gmail.com**. We will verify
your request using the email associated with your account and respond within the
time required by applicable law. You may use an authorized agent where permitted.
